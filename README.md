One invented English word plus its definition in one line (105 characters, limit 140). Top three take the $5 pot.

## Why this line

The word fills a real gap everyone knows — opening a tab and instantly forgetting why — and it is sayable, spellable and memorable (tab + amnesia). The definition lands in one reading and the last clause (the other 86 tabs watching in silence) gives it the reversal.

## Run it

node main.ts
